<title>4. AI Guidance Feature</title>
<description>The AI Guidance module processes user text inputs to map queries to relevant verses or contextual analysis. You agree not to submit sensitive personal data or malicious prompts into the generative AI engine.</description>

<title>5. Termination & Account Deletion</title>
<description>The App operates as a federated client within the unified Quran Foundation connected application ecosystem. Because your identity profile and authentication tokens are shared globally across multiple network services, a native "Delete Account" control is intentionally excluded from the <bold>DHAKIR</bold> app interface. This infrastructure guardrail is implemented to prevent users from inadvertently breaking credentials or corrupting data states across other synchronized client applications within the shared ecosystem.</description>
